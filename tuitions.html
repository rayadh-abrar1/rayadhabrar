How to Ace Your Exams

Proven strategies from someone who scored 6 A*s at O Levels and 3 As at A Levels.

O Levels

Acing O Levels

  • 01
    Past Papers Are Everything — Start doing past papers from the beginning, not the end. Pattern recognition is more valuable than memorising notes.
  • 02
    Build the Mark Scheme Mindset — Learn exactly what examiners want. Keywords, specific phrasing, and command words (state, explain, describe) are the difference between A and A*.
  • 03
    Physics & Maths: Derive, Don't Memorise — Understanding where formulas come from makes you faster and more accurate under pressure.
  • 04
    Consistent Short Sessions Beat Cramming — 45 minutes daily, 6 days a week beats 8 hours the night before. Build a revision timetable from Week 10 onwards.
  • 05
    Chemistry: Colour-Code Your Notes — Reactions, conditions, and observations in different colours. Visual memory is underrated in O Level Chemistry.
A Levels

Acing A Levels

  • 01
    Conceptual Depth Over Surface Coverage — A Level examiners test understanding, not recall. If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough.
  • 02
    Physics: Master the Six Big Topics — Mechanics, Electricity, Waves, Fields, Nuclear, and Thermal. Every question links back to these. Learn the connections between them.
  • 03
    Maths: Never Skip Steps — Method marks are your safety net. Show every line of working. Examiners award marks for process, not just answers.
  • 04
    Timed Mock Exams from Month 1 — Start timing yourself immediately. Time management at A Level is as important as knowledge.
  • 05
    Chemistry: Mechanisms Are Non-Negotiable — Organic chemistry mechanisms (arrows, curly arrows, conditions) require repetition. Draw them daily until they're automatic.
IB

Acing IB Mathematics

  • 01
    IA Starts Early — Your Internal Assessment counts for 20%. Start topic exploration in Year 1. Leaving it to Year 2 is the biggest IB mistake students make.
  • 02
    HL vs SL: Know the Extra Chapters — HL adds calculus depth, complex numbers, and proof by induction. Master these early — they appear in Paper 3.
  • 03
    GDC Mastery Is Mandatory — Your graphing calculator is a tool, not a crutch. Know how to use every relevant function before the mock exams.
  • 04
    Paper 1 (No Calculator): Speed Drills — Practice mental arithmetic and algebraic manipulation daily. 5 minutes every morning makes a measurable difference.
  • 05
    EE and TOK: Don't Leave 3 Points on the Table — The matrix bonus can push you across a grade boundary. Submit quality work for both.
