Curricula & experience

What I teach

Maths and sciences

Mathematics, physics and chemistry for Pearson Edexcel and Cambridge O Level and IGCSE students. I also support A Level learners in maths and physics and have tutored IB students.

My experience includes students from Sir John Wilson School, The Aga Khan School in Dhaka, Scholastica, SRU and SRM, plus international students learning online.

How sessions work

I start by checking what the student understands, teach the missing steps with worked examples, and then ask them to solve a similar problem independently.

For exam preparation, I use topic practice, timed past-paper questions, and marking feedback to decide what to work on next. I’ve developed O Level chemistry resources and materials for English essay writing.
